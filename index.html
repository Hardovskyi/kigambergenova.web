01 — Japan · Study Abroad

I learned to see before I learned to respond.

Six cities, dozens of buildings, a daily sketch. Studying SANAA, Kengo Kuma, Sou Fujimoto, and Tadao Ando in person — rather than through photographs — changed how I read space: how light enters a room, how a threshold shifts what the body expects, how restraint can say more than expression.

02 — Habitat for Humanity ReStore · Houston, TX

Architecture begins with listening.

A week of demolition, patching, painting, and landscaping inside homes that would soon belong to families. No drawings — a crew, a site, a set of tasks, and people who needed help. It taught me what buildings hold for the people who live in them, and what it means to show up and do the work without being asked to design anything.

03 — Design-Build Studio · Maharashtra, India

Concept to a built community room in eight weeks.

Design-build moved design out of the studio and into coordination, budget, labor, weather, and construction sequence. On an eight-week timeline and a $50k budget, a mid-project site relocation forced me to redesign the foundations and structural grid in five days — and we still delivered a finished community room. It taught me to make decisions that can be communicated clearly and adjusted fast.
